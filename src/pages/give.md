# How to Give
Sprouting Tech Foundation is a 501(c)3 public charity and does accept contributions. Individual donations made to the Foundation are tax deductible to the full extent of the law.

The Foundation will acknowledge contributions by letter or email, which also serves as an acknowledgement of the contribution for tax purposes.

To donate online, we are working on a donation link which will be available soon.

To donate by check, wire, or other methods, please reach out to us at donations@sproutingtech.org and we will provide instructions.

If you are interested in providing an in-kind donation, please reach out to us at donations@sprougingtech.org to discuss further.
